Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common CareerConnect questions.

Open the Jobs page, choose a suitable job and click the Apply button. Complete the application details and submit the application.
CareerConnect compares your profile information, skills, qualification, experience, location and job preferences with available jobs and calculates a matching percentage.
Open your Profile page and use the Resume Upload section. PDF and DOCX resume files can be analyzed when the required extraction packages are installed.
Open Applications from your dashboard. You can view the application status such as Applied, Under Review, Shortlisted, Interview and Selected.
Always verify government recruitment information through the official recruitment department or official notification before applying.
Employers can use the employer dashboard to find job seekers and view AI-based candidate matching against their posted jobs.
